## Dark Circles as the Main Eye Concern

When the user's primary complaint is under-eye dark circles or uneven pigmentation without emphasizing wrinkles or dehydration, a brightening-focused eye contour product is the more specific match.

This should be separated from puffiness-dominant complaints and from retinol-focused anti-aging treatment.

Infinity product match

Primary: Whitening Eye Contour — targeted eye-area product for dark circles and uneven under-eye tone.

It can also provide supportive hydration and puffiness care, but pigmentation is the clearest reason to select it.

## Dehydrated Under-Eye Area

An under-eye area that mainly feels dry, dehydrated, or lacks plumpness requires a hydration-centered approach rather than automatically using retinol or a pigmentation-focused treatment.

Hydration-focused eye care is particularly relevant when the user describes dryness together with a tired appearance or fine dehydration lines.

Infinity product match

Primary: Hyalu Collagen Eye Contour — hydration-focused eye treatment containing hyaluronic acid and marine collagen.

It is the preferred eye-area match when dehydration and plumping are more important than pigmentation or intensive anti-aging treatment.

## Dark Circles With Puffiness

Dark circles and puffiness can occur together, but product selection should depend on which feature is dominant.

When pigmentation or uneven under-eye tone is the main concern, a brightening-focused eye contour is the more direct choice.

When swelling, bags, or puffiness are more prominent, a caffeine-centered eye product may be more appropriate.

Infinity product match

Dark-circle dominant: Whitening Eye Contour.

Puffiness dominant: Eye Contour Cream Vitamin E & Caffeine.

Do not automatically recommend both products when one concern clearly dominates.

## Dark Circles With Under-Eye Dehydration

Dark circles may coexist with dry or dehydrated under-eye skin.

When pigmentation remains the main concern, brightening-focused eye care should remain the primary match.

When dehydration, dryness, and lack of plumpness are more prominent than pigmentation, a hydration-focused eye product may be more appropriate.

Infinity product match

Pigmentation dominant: Whitening Eye Contour.

Dehydration dominant: Hyalu Collagen Eye Contour.

## Tired-Looking Eyes With Dehydration

A tired under-eye appearance may result from a combination of puffiness and lack of hydration rather than pigmentation alone.

When the user emphasizes dryness, dehydration, puffiness, and a tired appearance together, hydration with depuffing support is a better match than a pigmentation-focused product.

Primary: Hyalu Collagen Eye Contour — appropriate for dehydrated, tired-looking eyes with puffiness.

## Dark Circles Versus Under-Eye Fine Lines

Dark circles and under-eye fine lines are distinct treatment goals even when they occur in the same area.

When pigmentation or uneven tone is the dominant concern, a brightening-focused eye product is more relevant.

When wrinkles and visible aging are the dominant concern, an eye-area retinol treatment is more targeted.

Infinity product match

Dark circles and uneven tone: Whitening Eye Contour.

Under-eye fine lines and wrinkles: Retinol Eye Cream.

Do not select Retinol Eye Cream solely because the concern is located around the eyes.
