## Recurrent Blackheads, Whiteheads, and Clogged Pores

Blackheads and whiteheads are forms of comedonal acne associated with persistent follicular congestion. When they repeatedly appear across an acne-prone area, treatment should focus on reducing pore blockage and preventing new comedones rather than treating each lesion individually.

Retinoid-based treatment is relevant to recurrent clogged pores because it supports skin turnover and helps reduce formation of new comedones. Salicylic acid can provide supportive pore decongestion.

Infinity product match:

- Primary: Retinol B3 Serum — the main Infinity match for recurrent blackheads, whiteheads, clogged pores, and uneven acne-prone texture.

- Supportive exfoliation: Micro Exfoliating Purifying Toner when surface congestion or uneven texture is also important.

## One or a Few Isolated Inflamed Pimples

A small number of individual red or inflamed pimples is different from widespread recurrent acne. When only one or a few isolated lesions are present, localized treatment directly on the active blemish is more appropriate than automatically applying a stronger acne treatment across the entire face.

Infinity product match:

- Primary: Intense Spot Cream — localized treatment for individual active acne blemishes.

Picking or squeezing an inflamed pimple can prolong inflammation and increase the likelihood of visible marks or scarring. Use a localized treatment instead of repeated manual manipulation.

## Acne Requiring Medical Evaluation

Some acne presentations are beyond the expected role of cosmetic skincare. Medical evaluation should take priority when acne is deep and painful, repeatedly produces permanent scars, remains significant despite consistent appropriate skincare, or requires established medical treatments not represented by the Infinity cosmetic catalog.

Infinity product guidance:

- Do not position an Infinity cosmetic product as the primary treatment for deep nodular or cystic acne.

- Do not present Infinity cosmetic products as treatments for significant structural acne scarring.

- Do not force another Infinity product when appropriate cosmetic or over-the-counter skincare has already failed.

- When the scientifically appropriate treatment is outside the Infinity catalog, state that no direct Infinity equivalent is available.

## Hormonal-Pattern Acne

Acne that repeatedly flares around the menstrual cycle, clusters mainly along the jawline or lower face, or begins suddenly in adulthood may have an important hormonal component.

Topical skincare may improve visible lesions but does not correct an underlying hormonal driver.

Infinity product guidance:

- Infinity acne products may provide supportive topical care according to the visible lesion pattern.

- No Infinity cosmetic product should be described as treating the underlying hormonal cause.

Persistent or significant hormonal-pattern acne should be medically assessed.
