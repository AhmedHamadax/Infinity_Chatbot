## Fine Lines and Wrinkles Overview

Fine lines and wrinkles can become more visible as skin renewal slows, hydration decreases, and the skin gradually loses some of its smoothness and elasticity.

The appropriate skincare approach depends on what dominates the presentation. Retinol-based treatment is more directly targeted when persistent wrinkles and visible aging are the main concern, while hydration-focused products are more appropriate when fine lines mainly appear together with dehydration, dryness, or loss of plumpness.

Infinity product match

No single product should automatically be recommended for every type of fine line.

Persistent facial fine lines and wrinkles: Retinol B3 Serum or Retinol B3 Cream depending on the desired product format.

Dehydration-related fine lines: Hyalu Collagen Serum.

Under-eye fine lines: use an eye-specific product rather than a full-face treatment.
