# Dehydrated Skin Skincare Recommendation Knowledge Base

## Skin concern
- Dehydrated skin / dry-dehydrated skin
- Signs mentioned include:
  - Fine lines
  - Itchiness
  - Sunken eyes
  - Cracked lips
  - Dry lips or mouth
- Article distinguishes dehydrated skin from dry skin:
  - **Dry skin:** associated with insufficient sebum
  - **Dehydration:** associated primarily with inadequate fluid levels or fluid loss

## Recommended product categories
- Gentle/exfoliating cleanser
- Hydrating serum
- Moisturizer
- Moisturizing SPF
- Hydrating face mask
- Chemical exfoliant for skin cycling
- Retinoid for skin cycling
- Peptide-rich serum

## Main treatment goals
- Increase and maintain skin hydration
- Support and nourish the skin barrier
- Reduce dryness
- Remove dead skin cells
- Improve complexion
- Soothe the skin
- Support collagen and elastin
- Allow recovery periods between active ingredients

## Important routine recommendations
- Consider **skin cycling** to avoid continuously exposing the skin barrier to potent active ingredients:
  - **Night 1:** Cleanse → chemical exfoliant such as salicylic or glycolic acid → moisturizer
  - **Night 2:** Cleanse → retinoid → moisturizer
  - **Nights 3–4:** Cleanse → peptide-rich hydrating serum → moisturizer
- An overnight nourishing mask may be added during recovery nights if extra comfort is needed.
- Look for hydrating cleanser ingredients such as **apricot oil**.
- Hydrating serums may contain:
  - Peptides
  - Omega fatty acids
  - Hyaluronic acid
- Look for moisturizers containing vitamins, minerals and peptides.
- Incorporate a moisturizing SPF into the morning routine.
- Reapply sunscreen every **2 hours** when spending time outdoors.
- Maintain adequate water intake.
- Use a hydrating face mask approximately **once weekly**.

## Cautions or limitations mentioned
- Dehydrated skin may result from insufficient fluid intake or increased fluid loss from illness, sunburn, sweating, excessive heat exposure or other health conditions.
- Skin cycling includes recovery nights specifically to avoid overwhelming the skin barrier with active ingredients.
- The article describes chemical exfoliation as gentler than physical scrubs and recommends it in place of physical exfoliation within the skin-cycling routine.
