## SECTION 1 — BRAND OVERVIEW

ALLURE is a premium medical-inspired skincare brand focused on
evidence-led formulations for the MENA market. All formulations are
developed for warm, humid climates and a range of skin tones. Products are
cruelty-free and fragrance options are clearly labeled. The house rule
across the range: introduce one active at a time, patch test first, and
always pair daytime actives with sun protection.

## SECTION 6 — INGREDIENT COMPATIBILITY & SAFETY RULES

*These rules live here, NOT in the individual product entries. Always
cross-reference.*

- **Retinol + Vitamin C:** Do not layer in the same routine. Use Vitamin C
  in the morning and any Renew Retinol product at night.
- **Retinol + Moisturizer (Hydra Boost):** Safe to layer; in fact
  recommended to buffer retinol for sensitive users.
- **Vitamin C Drops (20%) + any exfoliant:** Do not combine on the same
  day; high-concentration ascorbic acid plus exfoliation may irritate.
- **Any product coded "-B" in the VC-1150 family:** fragrance-free; these
  are the reformulations intended for reactive skin and should still be
  patch tested.
- **SPF timing:** Apply Daily Defense SPF 50 as the final morning step, but
  not immediately on top of wet Vitamin C serum — wait until absorbed.
- **Retinol + Pregnancy:** No Renew Retinol product (any strength) should be
  used during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

## SECTION 7 — USAGE PROTOCOLS BY GOAL

### 7.1 Anti-aging routine
Morning: Vitamin C → Moisturizer → SPF. Night: Retinol (start with the
Starter strength) → Moisturizer. Build retinol frequency gradually.

### 7.2 Brightening / dark spots routine
Morning: Vitamin C (choose by skin type per Section 2) → SPF. Consistency
over 4–6 weeks is required for visible change.

### 7.3 Sensitive-skin routine
Use only fragrance-free products. Avoid the 1.0% retinol and the 20%
Vitamin C Drops. Favor the Sensitive Vitamin C Serum and the Micellar Water.

## SECTION 10 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: How soon will I see results?**
Tone and texture improvements are typically visible within 4 to 6 weeks of
consistent use.

**Q: Which products are fragrance-free?**
Only the Vitamin C Serum Sensitive (VC-1150-B) is fully fragrance-free in
the Vitamin C range. See Section 6 for the suffix rule.

**Q: I'm new to retinol — where do I start?**
Begin with the lowest strength available and increase gradually. See Section
3.1 for the starter product and Section 7.1 for the routine.

**Q: Can I use two serums at once?**
Yes, but introduce actives one at a time and respect the compatibility rules
in Section 6 — especially retinol and Vitamin C, which must not be layered.

**Q: Do you ship free?**
Free shipping applies to Cairo and Giza orders over 1000 EGP. See Section 9.
